Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

DIPO LABS respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you use DIPO LABS, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Our privacy approach is based on data minimization, transparency, security, and local-first functionality wherever practical.

1. Information We Handle

DIPO LABS is designed to minimize unnecessary collection of personal information.

Depending on the features you use, information that may be handled can include:

  • Information you voluntarily enter into a tool.
  • Preferences required to operate parts of the website.
  • Recently used items or favorites stored on your device.
  • Technical information required for basic website operation.

Privacy principle: DIPO LABS does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information simply because it is available.

2. Local Storage & Preferences

Some DIPO LABS features may use browser storage such as localStorage to remember information on your device.

This can include preferences, recently used tools, favorites, interface settings, or other non-sensitive application data.

Local data normally remains in your browser unless you clear it or a feature specifically sends information to an external service.

3. DIPO LABS Tools & AI Features

DIPO LABS may provide tools that generate, transform, analyze, preview, or process content.

When a feature operates entirely in your browser, the relevant processing may occur locally on your device.

Some future or optional features may require an external service or API to process a request. When this happens, information necessary for that feature may be transmitted to the relevant service.

You should avoid entering passwords, payment information, private keys, confidential documents, or other sensitive information into tools unless the feature explicitly requires and securely supports it.

4. Analytics

DIPO LABS may use privacy-conscious analytics in the future to understand general website performance and usage.

Analytics, when enabled, should be used to improve the service rather than to collect unnecessary sensitive information.

Analytics should not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through normal website usage.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

DIPO LABS may use cookies or browser technologies when necessary for functionality, preferences, security, or optional analytics.

You can generally control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

Certain DIPO LABS features may rely on third-party services, APIs, hosting providers, analytics providers, or other infrastructure.

When a third-party service is required, the information sent to that service will depend on the feature being used and the service's own privacy practices.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review those policies when using features that depend on external services.

7. Security

DIPO LABS is designed with reasonable security practices appropriate to the website and its features.

We aim to reduce unnecessary data collection and avoid storing sensitive information when it is not required.

However, no internet service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

DIPO LABS does not intentionally design its services to collect unnecessary personal information from children.

If you believe that personal information has been provided through DIPO LABS inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information associated with your use of a service.

You may also be able to:

  • Clear locally stored website data from your browser.
  • Disable optional cookies or analytics through available controls or browser settings.
  • Stop using a feature that requires information you do not wish to provide.
  • Contact DIPO LABS with privacy-related questions or requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DIPO LABS introduces new features, changes its services, or needs to reflect legal or operational requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

11. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact DIPO LABS through the official contact method provided on the website.
