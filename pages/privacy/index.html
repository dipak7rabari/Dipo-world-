01 · Overview

Privacy at DIPO WORLD

DIPO WORLD is a browser-based digital tools platform focused on creative, productivity and customization experiences.

This Privacy Policy explains the general approach DIPO WORLD takes toward information, browser technologies, user-provided content, generated results and third-party services that may be associated with the platform.

Privacy principle

DIPO WORLD aims to collect, process and retain only information that is reasonably necessary for the operation, security, improvement or support of its services, where applicable.

By using DIPO WORLD, you acknowledge that digital services may involve browser storage, network requests, third-party technologies or other technical mechanisms depending on the feature being used.

02 · Information

Information You Provide

Different DIPO WORLD tools may allow you to enter or provide information such as text, usernames, links, QR content, contact details, Wi-Fi information, UPI-related information, images or other material required by a particular tool.

Examples of information

✍️ Text & usernames Text entered into DIPO BIO and related customization tools.
🔗 Links & URLs Information supplied for QR generation or sharing.
📷 Images Images may be supplied to features that support image processing.
📱 Contact details Information voluntarily entered into supported QR or profile tools.

You should avoid entering highly sensitive, confidential or unnecessary personal information into a tool unless the feature specifically requires it and you understand how that information will be processed.

03 · Browser Tools

Browser-Based Processing

DIPO WORLD includes tools designed to perform functionality directly within the user's browser. Depending on the feature, processing may occur locally on the device rather than being sent to a DIPO WORLD server.

01
You enter content Text, links, settings or other supported information.
02
Tool processes it The browser or applicable service performs the requested action.
03
You receive output The resulting bio, QR, barcode or other tool output.
Important technical note

Browser-based processing does not guarantee that no network request will ever occur. External services, hosting, fonts, integrations or future functionality may introduce network communication.

04 · Storage

Cookies & Browser Storage

DIPO WORLD features may use browser technologies such as local storage, session storage, cookies or similar mechanisms when required to provide functionality.

Why browser storage may be used

  • To remember interface preferences.
  • To preserve temporary tool settings.
  • To remember favorites or user-selected options.
  • To improve the usability of a browser-based tool.
  • To support security or technical functionality where applicable.

Browser storage can generally be managed through your browser settings. Clearing website data may remove locally stored preferences or saved tool information.

Your browser controls local storage

Information stored locally in your browser is subject to the storage controls and privacy settings of your browser and device.

05 · User Content

Content You Choose to Submit

DIPO WORLD tools may allow users to enter text, links, images, logos, profile information or other content for processing or generation.

You remain responsible for the content you submit, process, download, publish or distribute through the platform.

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information

Users should avoid submitting passwords, private keys, authentication codes, financial credentials, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information unless a feature explicitly requires such information.

⚠️ Use appropriate judgment

DIPO WORLD tools are intended for general creative, productivity and customization purposes. Always review what you enter before submitting it to any online service.

06 · Generated Results

Generated Content & Tool Output

DIPO WORLD may generate or transform text layouts, symbols, QR codes, barcodes, formatting styles and other outputs based on information supplied by the user.

Generated results may depend on browser capabilities, third-party libraries, algorithms, external services and the information supplied by the user.

Review before publishing

Users should review generated results before publishing, printing, selling, distributing or otherwise relying on them.

A generated result does not automatically grant ownership of third-party trademarks, logos, symbols, copyrighted material or other protected content contained within the user's input.

Simple rule

If you provide the input, you are responsible for making sure you have the appropriate rights or permission to use it.

07 · DIPO BIO

DIPO BIO Privacy

DIPO BIO provides tools for creating stylish bios, usernames, symbols, kaomoji, templates, fonts and profile-ready text.

Text entered into browser-based DIPO BIO features may be processed directly within the browser depending on the specific feature.

  • Stylish font conversion.
  • Kaomoji and symbol generation.
  • Bio formatting and remixing.
  • Preview and customization.
  • Copy-ready profile text.

Users should avoid entering passwords, private credentials or other confidential information into public-facing bio tools.

08 · DIPO QR

DIPO QR Privacy

DIPO QR provides QR and barcode creation features for supported content types including URLs, text, phone information, Wi-Fi information, UPI-related content and social-platform presets.

QR codes and barcodes are representations of the information supplied by the user. The generated code itself does not necessarily transmit or verify the information encoded within it.

Before sharing a QR code

Check the encoded information carefully. A QR code can contain links, contact details or other information that becomes accessible to anyone who scans it.

Third-Party Technologies

DIPO WORLD may use third-party libraries, browser APIs, hosting services, fonts, icons, integrations or other technologies to provide or improve functionality.

Third-party services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party names and brands

References to third-party platforms, social networks, brands, services or technologies do not necessarily mean that DIPO WORLD is affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by those organizations.

Users should review the applicable privacy policy of any third-party service they choose to use through or alongside DIPO WORLD.

10 · Technical Data

Technical & Device Information

Like most websites, access to DIPO WORLD may involve automatically generated technical information through browsers, hosting infrastructure, security systems or third-party services.

Depending on the infrastructure being used, this may potentially include information such as:

  • IP address or approximate network information.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device or operating-system information.
  • Requested pages or resources.
  • Date and time of requests.
  • Basic technical error information.

Such information may be handled by hosting providers, security systems or infrastructure providers according to their applicable policies.

11 · Security

Security & Protection

DIPO WORLD aims to use reasonable technical and organizational practices appropriate to a browser-based digital platform.

🔒 Secure delivery The platform may be delivered through HTTPS-enabled hosting and infrastructure.
🧩 Technical controls Available security and platform controls may be used where appropriate.
🛠️ Maintenance Technical issues and vulnerabilities may be addressed as they are identified.
⚠️ No absolute guarantee No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Users should also take reasonable precautions, including keeping devices and browsers updated and avoiding submission of unnecessary confidential information.

12 · Retention

Data Retention

The amount of time information may remain available depends on how the information is processed and where it is stored.

Information processed locally in a browser may remain only within the browser environment until the user clears it, closes the relevant session or otherwise removes it.

Information handled by hosting providers, third-party services or future DIPO WORLD infrastructure may be subject to their respective retention practices.

Minimal-retention approach

Where practical, DIPO WORLD aims to avoid retaining user-provided information longer than reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

13 · Children

Children's Privacy

DIPO WORLD is a general-purpose digital tools platform and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should not provide unnecessary personal information belonging to another person, including a child, through the platform.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, you may contact DIPO WORLD through the available official contact channel.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information handled by a service.

  • Request information about applicable data handling.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information where applicable.
  • Request deletion where legally applicable.
