FAQ

Before you contact us.

A few quick answers.

Is DIPO WORLD free to use?
DIPO WORLD includes free tools and features. Some future products or advanced features may have separate plans.
Does DIPO BIO support stylish Unicode text?
Yes. DIPO BIO is designed for stylish Unicode text, bios, usernames, symbols, kaomoji and creative profile content.
Can I create QR codes with DIPO QR?
Yes. DIPO QR is designed for creating custom QR experiences including links, text and other supported formats.
